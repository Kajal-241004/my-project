# 🌾 कृषि साथी (Krishi Saathi) - AI Kisan Dost

> **हर कदम किसान के साथ | Powered by Google Antigravity + Gemini API**  
> *A high-grade, mobile-first Web Application designed specifically for Indian farmers in Hindi + Hinglish.*

---

## 👨‍🌾 About the Project

**Krishi Saathi (कृषि साथी - AI किसान दोस्त)** is an intelligent agricultural companion web application built with a vibrant **Green & Yellow farmer theme**, large touch-friendly buttons, and high accessibility features (voice narration, speech recognition, and instant disease diagnosis). It empowers farmers with real-time market rates, plant disease detection, weather-based irrigation alerts, and government subsidy calculations.

**Crafted with ❤️ by Kajal Gautam (Solo) with Google Antigravity + Gemini API.**

## 📱 Key Features & Pages

### 1. 🏠 Home Page (Namaste Kisan!)
* **Personalized Greeting**: *"नमस्ते किसान भाई! 🙏"* with time-aware salutations.
* **Live Status Pill**: Quick summary of today's temperature (32°C), rain probability (70%), and tomato rates in Padrauna.
* **4 Big Interactive Cards**:
  1. 🩺 **फसल डॉक्टर (Fasal Doctor)** - Crop disease scanner & remedy
  2. 🌾 **मंडी भाव (Mandi Bhav)** - Live wholesale & retail vegetable rates
  3. ⛅ **मौसम व सलाह (Mausam & Advisory)** - 32°C, 70% rain alert
  4. 📜 **योजना दोस्त (Yojna Dost)** - Government subsidy & KCC loans
* **Kisan Toll-Free Helpline**: One-touch call button for Kisan Call Center (`1800-180-1551`).

### 2. 🩺 फसल डॉक्टर (Fasal Doctor - AI Plant Pathologist)
* **Crop Photo Upload**: Take a photo using your mobile camera or upload from gallery.
* **Sample Crop Previews**: Quick 1-click test diagnosis for:
  * 🍅 **Tamatar (Tomato)**: *Tamatar me Patti Mudna Rog (Tomato Leaf Curl Virus)*
  * 🥔 **Aloo (Potato)**: *Aloo me Jhulsa Rog (Late Blight of Potato)*
  * 🌾 **Gehu (Wheat)**: *Gehu me Peela Ratua (Yellow Rust)*
* **Detailed Diagnosis & Prescription**:
  * Disease Name & Cause (e.g., Whitefly transmission)
  * Severity Rating (Medium / High)
  * Recommended Medicines & Estimated Prices:
    * **इमिडाक्लोप्रिड (Imidacloprid 17.8% SL)** - ₹180 / 100ml
    * **नीम का तेल (Neem Oil 10000 PPM)** - ₹160 / 250ml
  * Precise Dosage instructions & safety precautions.
* **🤖 Ask Gemini AI**: Interactive AI chat powered by Google Gemini to ask follow-up questions about fertilizers, tonics, and organic remedies.

### 3. 🌾 मंडी भाव (Mandi Bhav - Live Market Rates)
* **🔴 LIVE UPDATE Badge**: Certified daily rates from e-NAM & APMC.
* **Mandi Switcher**:
  * **पडरौना मंडी (Padrauna Mandi, Kushinagar, UP)** [Primary]
  * **गोरखपुर मंडी (Gorakhpur APMC)**
  * **देवरिया मंडी (Deoria APMC)**
* **Real Commodity Rates**:
  * 🍅 **Tamatar (टमाटर)**: **₹20 / kg** (₹2,000 / qtl) [🔺 +₹2 आज तेज]
  * 🥔 **Aloo (आलू - नया)**: **₹15 / kg** (₹1,500 / qtl) [🔻 -₹1 गिरावट]
  * 🧅 **Pyaz (प्याज)**: **₹25 / kg** (₹2,500 / qtl) [● स्थिर भाव]
  * 🌶️ **Hari Mirch (हरी मिर्च)**: ₹40 / kg
  * 🥦 **Gobhi (फूलगोभी)**: ₹18 / kg
  * 🌾 **Gehu (गेहूं MSP)**: ₹2,275 / क्विंटल
  * 🌾 **Dhan (धान)**: ₹2,183 / क्विंटल
  * 🎋 **Ganna (गन्ना UP SAP)**: ₹370 / क्विंटल
* **Search & Filters**: Instant filter by *Sabzi (सब्जियां)*, *Anaj (अनाज)*, or *Nakdi Fasal (नकदी फसल)*.

### 4. ⛅ मौसम व कृषि सलाह (Mausam & Irrigation Advisory)
* **Current Weather Card**: **32°C**, Feels like 35°C, Padrauna (UP).
* **🌧️ 70% Rain Probability Alert**.
* **⚠️ "कल पानी मत डालो" (Do Not Irrigate Tomorrow!) Warning**:
  * Prominent red/amber alert warning farmers not to irrigate or spray chemical pesticides over the next 24 hours to prevent root rot and wash-off.
* **5-Day Farming Forecast**: Tailored day-by-day advice on soil moisture, spraying feasibility, and harvest readiness.

### 5. 📜 योजना दोस्त (Yojna Dost - Government Schemes)
* **Top Farmer Welfare Schemes**:
  1. **PM Kisan Samman Nidhi**: ₹6,000/year (3 installments of ₹2,000).
  2. **Kisan Credit Card (KCC)**: Up to ₹3 Lakh loan at 4% effective interest.
  3. **PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**: 100% loss compensation against drought or flood.
  4. **PM KUSUM Solar Pump**: Up to 90% government subsidy on solar tube-wells.
* **Interactive Eligibility Calculator (पात्रता जांचें)**:
  * Enter land size (Acres/Bigha) and farmer category.
  * Instant breakdown of approved schemes, estimated annual economic benefits, document checklist (Aadhaar, Khatauni, Bank Passbook), and direct portal links.

### 6. 🎤 आवाज़ सहायक (Voice Assistant - बोलो किसान, क्या पूछना है?)
* **Giant Pulsing Microphone Button** for effortless touch interaction.
* **Web Speech Recognition**: Speaks and listens in Hindi & Hinglish (`hi-IN`).
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Reads out answers in natural Hindi audio.
* **Quick Tap Common Questions**:
  * *"Tamatar me patti mudna rog ki dawa kya hai?"*
  * *"Padrauna mandi me aloo aur tamatar ka kya rate hai?"*
  * *"Kisan Credit Card (KCC) kaise banwaye?"*
  * *"Kya kal baarish hogi? Khet me paani lagayein ya nahi?"*

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend**: React 18 (Hooks: `useState`, `useEffect`, `useRef`)
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom farmer color palette (Forest Green `#16a34a`, Golden Amber `#f59e0b`, Soil Brown)
* **Typography**: Google Fonts (*Noto Sans Devanagari* for clean Hindi text & *Poppins*)
* **Icons**: FontAwesome 6 Pro Agri & Lucide Icon set
* **AI Engine**: Google Gemini API Integration (`gemini-1.5-flash`)
* **Key Configuration**:
  ```javascript
  const GEMINI_API_KEY = "YOUR_API_KEY_HERE";
  ```
* **Offline / Fallback Intelligence**: Built-in agricultural intelligence engine so the app functions 100% smoothly even with the default key placeholder or when offline!

## 🚀 How to Run the App

### Option 1: One-Click Launch (Windows)
Double-click `run.bat` in the project folder. It will start the server and open `http://localhost:5173` automatically!

### Option 2: Run with Python HTTP Server
Open PowerShell or Terminal in the project folder:
```powershell
py server.py
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:5173`

### Option 3: Direct File Open (Zero Server Needed)
Double-click `standalone.html` in your browser (Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox). It runs instantly offline without any local server!
